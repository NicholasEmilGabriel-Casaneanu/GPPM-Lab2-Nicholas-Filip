Why is this hard?

The rule is trivial, yet proving every number reaches 1 has resisted every attempt since 1937. We can verify it for all n up to ~268, but that's not a proof.

Notice how even numbers fall fast (halving) while odd numbers spike (roughly 3×). The trajectory "hailstones" — it rises, falls, rises again, and lands on 1. The peak height grows with n but never seems to escape.
