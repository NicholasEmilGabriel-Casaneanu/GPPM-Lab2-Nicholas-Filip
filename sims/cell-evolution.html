A pond contains four competing cell species, each with a small genome. Within a species, individuals are genetically similar but carry small variations — you can tell them apart by their subtle colour shifts and behaviour. Feed by phagocytosis (absorbing nutrients through the membrane), defend with cell walls and toxins, and reproduce. When a stage ends, the majority gene of each surviving species becomes the new base genome for the next stage. If a species is wiped out, it's gone for good. You control the highlighted cell of your chosen species.
