What you can learn here

Each tab is a real simulation. Try things, break things, watch numbers change. In Truss, add joints and beams and watch forces flow to the supports — members turn red when they're overstressed. In Beam, drag the load to see how shear and moment change shape. In Circuit, click to place components and watch current flow through the wires. In Gears, spin the driver and see how tooth counts trade speed for torque. In Projectile, drag the launch vector and trace the arc.

Nothing is graded. Nothing can fail permanently. Every change is one undo away (right-click or press R to reset the current tab).
