How to interact

Truss: Drag from one joint to another to add a member. Drag joints to move them. Double-click a joint to toggle a support. Right-click a member to delete it. Pick a material, build within budget, then hit Test Bridge to see a vehicle drive across.

Beam: Click anywhere on the beam to add a point load. Drag loads to move them. Right-click to remove.

Circuit: Click a component to select it, then click another to wire them. Click a switch to toggle it. Click a selected component again to cycle its type.

Gears: Click empty space to add gears. Drag them near each other and they'll mesh. Use the +/− buttons on each gear to change its teeth count.

Projectile: Click-drag from the cannon to aim and set speed. Release to launch. The last 3 shots stay on screen to compare.
