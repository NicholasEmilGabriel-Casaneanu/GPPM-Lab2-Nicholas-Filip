The route to chaos

For r < 1 the population dies out. From r = 1 to 3, it settles to a single fixed point. At r = 3 the fixed point loses stability and the map settles into a 2-cycle. Then 4, 8, 16 — each doubling comes faster. The windows where r is at 3.5699… are infinite. Beyond that, chaos.

The cobweb plot below shows the iteration path: start at x₀, go up to the curve, across to the diagonal, up again, and so on. A fixed point means the cobweb spirals into a point. A cycle means it traces a closed loop. Chaos means it fills space.
