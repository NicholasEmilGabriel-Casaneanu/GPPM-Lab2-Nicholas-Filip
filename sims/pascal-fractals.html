Why Sierpiński?

The binomial coefficient C(n,k) counts subsets. When you color each entry by whether it's even, a Sierpiński triangle emerges — as if by magic. The reason: Lucas' Theorem. Write n and k in base 2; the coefficient is odd if and only if every binary digit of k is ≤ the corresponding digit of n. That condition traces exactly Sierpiński's shape.

Change the modulus and you get different fractals: mod 3 gives a three-way version, mod 5 a five-way one. The pattern is always self-similar — zoom in and you see the same structure repeating.
