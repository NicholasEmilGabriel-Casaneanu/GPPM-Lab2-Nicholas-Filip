Who wins and who loses?

A price ceiling (like rent control) helps the buyers who can find housing — but it creates a shortage: at the capped price, more people want the good than suppliers are willing to produce. Some are left out entirely.

A price floor (like minimum wage) helps the workers who keep their jobs — but it creates a surplus: more people want to work than employers want to hire. Some lose their jobs. Economists call the lost trades deadweight loss — value that neither side captures.
