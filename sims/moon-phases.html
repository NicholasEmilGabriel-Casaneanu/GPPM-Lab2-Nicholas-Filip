Drag the Moon around its orbit to see the phase it presents from Earth. Adjust the Moon's orbital tilt — the key parameter that determines whether eclipses can happen. When the Moon crosses the Earth-Sun line (the node line) while tilted slightly, the geometry lines up for a solar or lunar eclipse. The dashed lines in the canvas show Earth's shadow cone and the Moon's orbit, and the red/blue markers show the node line. Observe multiple angles and use Export data for a full report.
