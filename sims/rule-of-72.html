Doubling example

Where does 72 come from?

For small rates, ln(1 + r) ≈ r. So the exact doubling time is ln(2) / ln(1 + r) ≈ 0.693 / r. Multiply numerator and denominator by 100 to express r as a percent, and you get ≈ 69.3 / r%.

But 69 is awkward to divide in your head. 72 has many factors (2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 9, 12) and works well for the rates people actually see. It slightly overestimates the true answer below 10% and underestimates above 15% — but it's always within a fraction of a year.
