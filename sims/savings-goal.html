Time beats rate (almost) every time

Want $1M at 7%? In 40 years, you need $380/month. In 30 years, $850/month. In 20 years, $2,100/month. Waiting 10 extra years roughly halves the required monthly savings. Time is the most powerful ingredient in this formula.

Try raising the rate from 5% to 10% with a fixed horizon — the monthly payment drops, but not nearly as dramatically as doubling the horizon does. Compounding rewards patience more than it rewards return-chasing.
