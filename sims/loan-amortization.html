Why the crossover matters

Every payment contains some interest (a % of the remaining balance) plus some principal (the rest). Because interest is charged on the current balance, and that balance shrinks slowly at first, early payments are mostly interest. In a 30-year mortgage at 6%, you don't start paying more principal than interest until around year 12.

This is why extra payments early on are worth more than extra payments late: they attack the balance when the interest is largest. Try adding $200/month — you'll save tens of thousands and cut years off the loan.
