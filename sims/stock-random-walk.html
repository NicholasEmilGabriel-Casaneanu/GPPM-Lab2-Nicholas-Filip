Why the fan?

Each path is a different possible future. They all start at the same value, but each step is a random draw from a normal distribution scaled by volatility. Over time, small random shocks compound — so the distribution of outcomes widens. That widening is what we mean by "risk."

Notice that the median path ends up below the mean (the box on the left of the chart). That's because the distribution is right-skewed: a few paths soar (dragging the mean up), while most land modestly below. Higher volatility widens the fan but doesn't raise the median much.
