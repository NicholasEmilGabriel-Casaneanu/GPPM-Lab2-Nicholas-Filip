Why does compounding feel like magic?

Compound interest earns interest on interest. If you leave $1,000 at 7% for 40 years with no contributions, you end up with $14,974 — nearly 15× your money. But if you add $200/month, you end up with $483,000. Contributions early are worth far more than contributions late, because they have more time to compound.

Watch the shaded area (total you put in) vs the blue curve (what you have): the gap between them is pure interest. That gap is where the magic happens — and it only opens up in the last third of the timeline. Switch to Retirement and notice the curve is flat for 20 years, then rockets up.
