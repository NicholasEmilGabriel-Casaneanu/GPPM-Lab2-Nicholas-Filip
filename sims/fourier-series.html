What's the Gibbs phenomenon?

No matter how many harmonics you add, the square wave's corners always overshoot by about 9%. That's the Gibbs phenomenon: the Fourier series converges to the square wave everywhere except at the jump points, where it always ripples. Adding more harmonics makes the ripple narrower, but not shorter.

Switch to Sawtooth to see the ripples differently (sawtooth needs all harmonics), and Triangle to see that smooth shapes need far fewer terms.
