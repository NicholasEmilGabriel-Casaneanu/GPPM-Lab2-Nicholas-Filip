What you can learn here

A cube has 8 vertices, 12 edges, and 6 faces. Every Platonic solid satisfies Euler's formula: V − E + F = 2. Switch shapes and watch the counters update.

With the Distance and Angle tools you can click anywhere on the surface — a corner, the middle of an edge, the centre of a face, or a random point. Use Pick mode to choose whether clicks snap to a vertex, an edge, a face, or stay exact ("Any").

The Cut plane slices through the shape. The cross-section is drawn in orange; drag the ↕ handle or scroll while the Cut tool is active to slide it. A cube cut diagonally through its centre gives a hexagon; cut through a corner gives a triangle.

Tip: Zoom out until the shape looks tiny — the distance marker keeps its visual size, so you can always read the measurement without squinting.
